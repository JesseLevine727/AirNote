# DRAFT — AirNote rev 0.2 fabrication and assembly export

These files are generated by `hardware/tools/export_outputs.py` from the canonical rev 0.2 CAD. The script runs ERC and DRC first and refuses to export if either fails. This is not an approved production release, and nothing has been ordered. Before uploading, confirm that every file's SHA-256 matches `hardware/reports/final-artifact-hashes.json`, and that the review status in `reviews/REVIEW_STATUS.md` allows the order.

The copper finish is not set in CAD; it is chosen on the order form. ENIG is recommended for the 0.4 mm QFN, the LGA sensor and the exposed pads. Lead-free HASL is acceptable. This is the user's decision.

## CPL notes: check rotations in JLC's placement preview

- **Coordinates.** The CPL uses KiCad's own export unchanged: millimetres from KiCad's absolute origin, Y negative (KiCad's export convention), and KiCad rotations.
- **No rotation correction.** No rotation-correction table is applied, so every part must be checked in JLC's placement preview before paying. Pay particular attention to the polarized and pin-1 parts: U1, U2, U3, U4 (optical LGA), U5, D1, D2, Y1, SW3 and J1.
- **Parts whose footprint anchor is not the body centre.** Their preview markers sit at the anchor, not over the body:
  - **J1** (USB-C, rotation 180): anchor (64.00, 53.675). The pad field centre is about 1.1 mm further into the board (y 54.81), and the courtyard centre is at y 53.97. The shell stakes must drop into their slots.
  - **J2** (1×5 header, through-hole): anchor at pin 1 (112.0, 84.0). The body centre is 5.08 mm along the row, at y 89.08.
  - **J3** (JST PH 2-pin, through-hole): anchor at pin 1 (132.0, 104.0). The body centre is about 1 mm east, near (133.0, 104.5).
  - **RV1** (PTV09 potentiometer, through-hole): anchor at pin 1 (69.0, 108.0). The body and shaft centre (footprint fab outline) is at (76.0, 105.5).
- **Centred parts.** All other parts are anchored at their body centre.

## Upload steps (JLCPCB)

1. **PCB.** Upload `airnote-jlc-gerbers.zip`. Check that JLC's viewer shows 4 copper layers, the outline and both drill files.
2. **PCB options:**
   - Base material FR-4, **4 layers**, 90 × 70 mm, quantity 5.
   - **1.6 mm**. Impedance control: **No** (the USB geometry is calculated for the stackup, not ordered as a controlled-impedance service). Layer stackup **JLC04161H-7628**, JLC's default; if the form offers a stackup choice, select it. Re-check the code against `docs/fabrication_plan.md`.
   - Outer copper **1 oz**, inner copper **0.5 oz**.
   - Surface finish **ENIG** (recommended) or **lead-free HASL** (user's choice).
   - Via covering: **tented**, or **epoxy-filled and capped (POFV)** if the exposed-pad thermal vias under U1/U5 are to be filled (user's choice; see `docs/fabrication_plan.md`).
   - Minimum via 0.45 mm / 0.20 mm drill. Track and gap 0.15 / 0.127 mm.
   - **Remove order number: "Specify a location".** The bottom silkscreen carries `JLCJLCJLCJLC` at (70, 90) for the order number.
3. **PCBA options:**
   - **Standard PCBA**, assemble the **top side**, **2 of the 5 boards**.
   - Confirm part placement in the preview.
   - **No board washing.** The VL53L1X is an optical part. If the form has no option for this, add it as an order remark.
   - **Leave the VL53L1X protective liner on**; we remove it after assembly.
   - The through-hole parts (RV1, J2, J3) need JLC's through-hole assembly. The USB-C J1 is SMT; its 1.20 mm shell stakes solder into plated slots and do not pass through the 1.6 mm board.
4. **BOM / CPL.** Upload `airnote-jlc-bom.csv` and `airnote-jlc-cpl.csv`. Check every LCSC match against the BOM, re-check stock on the day (J1 C5184243 and RV1 C5710902 were low on 2026-10-10), and check every rotation and anchor in the placement preview (see above).
5. **Before paying.** Compare the quote's layer count, stackup, finish, via treatment and part list with this page.
